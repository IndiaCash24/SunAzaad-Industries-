Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding Google Play Store deployment, 14-day 20 testers policies, pricing, and our offline IT training in Bokaro.

How does Google Play's 14-day 20 testers closed testing work?
For personal developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, Google mandates that at least 20 testers must be continuously opted-in to your closed test for at least 14 days before applying for production access. SunAzaad Industries provisions 20 real physical devices with active testers who install, keep the app installed, and provide quality feedback logs to guarantee production approval.
What do I need to provide for Google Play app deployment?
To publish your application, we require your compiled Android App Bundle (.aab file), production Keystore file (.jks or credentials), app title, short & full description, 512x512 app icon, 1024x500 feature graphic, screenshot previews, and your privacy policy URL (which we can also generate and host for you).
What if Google rejects my initial application submission?
Our deployment services include comprehensive rejection mitigation. We analyze Google's review feedback, resolve policy violations (such as Data Safety disclosures, Target SDK version issues, or permission justifications), re-audit your build, and resubmit until your application successfully passes review.

Can you generate and host a privacy policy for my app?
Yes! Google Play strictly requires an active, publicly accessible HTTPS privacy policy URL. We generate a custom policy tailored to your app's exact permissions (camera, storage, location, ad networks) and host it securely with high-uptime infrastructure for ₹499.
